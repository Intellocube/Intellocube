What we collect

When you submit the contact form on this site, we collect the information you provide: your name, work email, service interest, estimated data volume, and any project details you enter. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and discuss a potential project.
  • To keep internal records of business inquiries.
  • We do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

Third-party services

Form submissions on this site are processed by FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service, which relays your message to our inbox. If website analytics are enabled, we may also use Google Analytics to understand aggregate site traffic; see the cookie notice on this site for details and your choices.

Cookies

This site uses a minimal, essential cookie/local-storage entry to remember your cookie-consent choice. If analytics are enabled, Google Analytics may set its own cookies to measure site usage after you consent — see the banner shown on your first visit.

Data retention

We retain contact form submissions for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain business records, after which they may be deleted.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. To make a request, email us at info@intellocube.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@intellocube.com.
